# k_study.txt - the matrices that decompose K, inside the model domain and at each of its limits.
#
# K = B * (Q / Q_real) * beta: the counted share of the real DRAM traffic times the share of the
# bandwidth the kernel keeps busy. Inside the domain the first factor is one within five per cent
# and K/K_ref is the ratio of the two betas. Each limit of the model is one factor leaving that
# regime, so each group below is chosen to move one factor and to leave the other alone.
